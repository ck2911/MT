\chapter*{Statement on the Use of AI Tools}
\thispagestyle{empty}

\noindent I used AI tools in preparing this thesis, specifically Claude (Anthropic) and, for retrieving the raw data, Codex (OpenAI). I used them for four purposes:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.8em, itemsep=0.3em, topsep=0.5em]
  \item \textbf{Code.} Writing, debugging and documenting the Python and R code for data construction, estimation, figures and tables, and the LaTeX source and build setup.
  \item \textbf{Building the analysis.} Implementing the empirical steps I specified, and checking that results, tables and text agree with each other.
  \item \textbf{Organising information.} Structuring my notes and literature, and checking references and data provenance against the original sources.
  \item \textbf{LaTeX design and text.} Designing the LaTeX layout, and suggesting wording for parts of the text, which I then revised.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent The research question, the empirical design, the choice of data, variables and specifications, the interpretation of the results and the conclusions are my own. I reviewed every result and every sentence against the underlying outputs before including it, and I take full responsibility for the content of this thesis, including any errors that arise from the use of AI tools.
